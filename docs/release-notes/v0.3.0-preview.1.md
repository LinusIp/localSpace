**Preview.** This is a preview of localSpace 0.3.0, for you to try before the release. Everything runs on your computer, and nothing you type leaves it.

## What's new

- **Two small models from Liquid AI, LFM3 1.7B and LFM3-VL 2B,** can be picked in the model list. LFM3-VL 2B also reads pictures. Each is about 1 to 1.5 GB. They're never chosen for you.
  - Their licence: "LFM Open License: free for personal use and for organisations under $10M yearly revenue; larger organisations need a licence from Liquid AI."
- **A newer engine** (llama.cpp b11960), which runs the LFM3 models.

## Known issues

- **Qwen3 30B A3B and gpt-oss 120B aren't offered in this preview.** They haven't been tested on the newer engine yet. localSpace 0.2.0 still offers them.
