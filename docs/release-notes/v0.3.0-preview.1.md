**Preview.** This is a preview of localSpace 0.3.0, for you to try before the release. Everything runs on your computer, and nothing you type leaves it.

## What's new

- **A new look.** New colours, a new typeface (Onest), and redrawn buttons, fields and panels.
  - **Settings → Appearance:** choose the colours ("Indigo and amber", "Violet and magenta" or "Petrol teal") and the mode ("Same as Windows", "Light" or "Dark"). Your choice is kept after a restart, and on an organisation's server each person chooses their own.
- **Two small models from Liquid AI, LFM3 1.7B and LFM3-VL 2B,** can be picked in the model list. LFM3-VL 2B also reads pictures. Each is about 1 to 1.5 GB. They're never chosen for you.
  - Their licence: "LFM Open License: free for personal use and for organisations under $10M yearly revenue; larger organisations need a licence from Liquid AI."
  - LFM3 1.7B works best in English and Russian; for Uzbek, Qwen3.5 is better.
- **A newer engine** (llama.cpp b11960), which runs the LFM3 models.

## Known issues

- **Qwen3 30B A3B and gpt-oss 120B aren't offered in this preview.** They haven't been tested on the newer engine yet. localSpace 0.2.0 still offers them.
- With Gemma 4 E4B, an answer carried on with Continue can stop partway. Starting a new message usually helps.
- **The app is mostly in English.** Full Russian and Uzbek come in a later version.
- **The installer isn't signed.**
  - If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
  - If Smart App Control blocks it, there is no way past that message. Please keep Smart App Control on.
  - Check your download against its SHA-256 below.
- In very long chats, especially in some languages, answers may be cut short. Starting a new chat helps.

## Downloads

- `localSpace-0.3.0-…-windows-x64-setup.exe`: the installer. It updates 0.2.0 and keeps your chats.
- `localSpace-0.3.0-…-windows-x64-portable.zip`: the same app, unpacked into a folder of your choice.
- Each file's SHA-256 is in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. To check a file, open PowerShell in your Downloads folder and run `Get-FileHash .\<file> -Algorithm SHA256`; if the two values differ, don't run it.

The licences of everything localSpace includes are in `licences\THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` beside the app.
